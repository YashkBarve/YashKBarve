## 🔬 Research

### Loan Defaulter Prediction Using Machine Learning

Authored and presented a peer-reviewed research paper at the **5th IEEE ICUIS 2025 conference**.

The study benchmarked:

- XGBoost
- Random Forest
- SVM

on **255,347 loan records**.

**Best reported result:**  
**91% Accuracy | 0.94 AUC-ROC**
